# Evidence and source history

The owner supplied `IMG_6107.HEIC` (modified mouse photo) and `IMG_6110.PNG` (web interface screenshot). The owner confirmed an ESP32-C3, 2N2222 transistor, Arduino IDE firmware and Wi-Fi web controls.

The original sketch, GPIO mapping, resistor values, schematic, construction date and video were not supplied. The earlier development chat was not recovered during publication. The photo alone cannot establish exact wiring.

`firmware/MouseAutoClicker/` is a newly written reference implementation. It is not recovered historical code, and no claim is made that it matches the original behavior exactly. Its defaults and safeguards are publication-time design choices.

The hardware photo was converted to JPEG for browser compatibility. Embedded camera/location metadata was removed from publication copies. The screenshot's visual content was preserved. No stock or generated project images were added.

The 20 publication commits organize real files and implementation work. They are dated when prepared, not backdated to the original build, and do not represent recovered development milestones.
